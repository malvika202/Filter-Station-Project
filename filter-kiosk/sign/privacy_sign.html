Filter Station

About this camera

What the camera does

It shows a live, mirror-image video on this screen and finds where faces are, so it can add a filter. It doesn't know who you are.

What happens to the video

No photos or video are saved, and nothing leaves this kiosk. Each frame is shown here, then replaced by the next. The only thing the filters save is a staff setup measurement: three numbers about face size, not a photo.

Who gets a filter

Only someone within about 5 feet who is facing the camera. Faces farther away, turned away, or hard to measure are blurred. Blurring only works on faces the camera finds, so it can miss one.

How to stay off screen

Stay out of the camera's view. Anyone in view shows up on this screen, even far back, though far faces are blurred.
